# Notices and a Wider Event Scope

Status: Accepted · 2026-11-19

Amends [ADR 0004](0004-event-filings-in-scope.md). The fixed category list now has two kinds. Event categories are listed, summarised and count toward the per-company cap of ten; besides ADR 0004's eight they add company outlook, shareholder value, shareholder meeting, operations, loans and guarantees, management matters and clarification. Notice categories (IR events, record dates, meeting notices, results-date notices, report notices, the compliance program, exchange inquiries and other notices) are shown only as a grouped count with links, need no body and are never summarised. A filing still gets exactly one category or one named exclusion by deterministic rules; ownership reports and SEC trading plans stay excluded until a structured insider and ownership section exists. This was chosen over keeping notices as exclusions because they are official company filings the owner wants visible, and over summarising them because they carry no content beyond a date and a title. The list's priority order, the notice kind and the rules are in `contracts/filing-categories.json` version 2; decision D76 has the type table.
